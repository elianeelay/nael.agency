Cleanframe

In-house Studio Project · Branding & Web Design

The Challenge

Many small and medium-sized businesses need a professional online presence but shy away from the cost and complexity of a traditional agency process. There was a gap for an offering that combines strong design quality with a fast, straightforward workflow.

The Idea

Cleanframe was developed as a standalone studio concept: clear, reduced websites for SMEs, built on a reusable system rather than starting from scratch with every project. The design language follows the name — black and white, precise, no visual noise. The website itself was meant to be the first proof of that quality promise.

The Execution

  • Standalone brand, logo, and visual system
  • Bilingual website (German/English) with automatic language detection
  • Clear, transparent service and pricing structure
  • Contact form with direct enquiry handling
  • Built lean for fast load times and easy maintenance
