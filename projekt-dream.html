A Different Dream for Every Blend.

Organic Herbal Tea Blends

Dream is a collection of three organic, caffeine-free herbal teas, each built around its own dreamscape. Rather than differentiating flavours with colour blocks or icons, every blend gets a fully illustrated world of its own — something to look at as much as to steep.

Ocean Dream drifts through a sunken city with mermaids and dolphins; Cosmic Dream floats among planets and whales adrift in deep space; Ancient Dream wanders an enchanted forest guarded by a dragon and a watchful stag. Each illustration grows out of a woman's silhouette, as if her hair itself were dreaming.

Deep jewel-tone tins — navy, aubergine, forest green — are finished with warm copper foil and refined serif type, giving each blend a collectible, gift-worthy presence that still feels calm and grounded rather than loud.

The result is packaging that does double duty as storytelling: pick a tin, and you're choosing which dream you want to step into tonight.

Our creative process starts with emotion — not with AI.

Every collection begins with an original artistic concept. Before AI becomes part of the workflow, we design the emotional experience we want each illustration to convey: its atmosphere, symbolism, storytelling, and visual identity.

Using digital sketches, concept art, and other creative techniques, we build the foundation of every piece. Only then do we integrate AI as a supporting tool to refine and expand the artwork.

No matter how realistic or detailed the final result becomes, our goal is always the same: to preserve the original feeling, artistic vision, and soul of the illustration. AI enhances our creative process — it never replaces it.
