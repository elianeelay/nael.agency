From Annual Report to Presentation System.

Presentation Design + Information Design + Visual Storytelling

BLOC Group required the transformation of an outdated corporate annual report into a premium presentation system for executive meetings, investor communications, and business development. The objective was to preserve the established corporate identity while improving clarity, readability, and visual impact for presentation use.

The target audience included executive leadership, investors, business partners, procurement teams, and industrial clients.

The project focused on redesigning complex editorial layouts into a cohesive presentation system with a clear visual hierarchy, simplified information architecture, and presentation-ready compositions. Data visualizations, KPI pages, infographics, and presentation layouts were restructured to improve communication while maintaining the technical credibility of the original content.

A recurring network motif was introduced as the central visual concept, symbolizing connectivity, collaboration, and integrated systems. Reappearing throughout the presentation, this visual language creates consistency across all slides while reinforcing the company's core values of partnership, organization, expertise, and innovation.

The final result is a contemporary corporate presentation system that combines bold typography, geometric compositions, industrial photography, and a cohesive design language to communicate engineering excellence, operational efficiency, and technological leadership.
