A Scalable Visual System Inspired by the Periodic Table.

Packaging + Product System + Visual Language

The supplement market is often dominated by crowded packaging, inconsistent product hierarchies, and aggressive colour coding. As product ranges expand, maintaining clarity and recognition becomes increasingly difficult.

The objective was to create a packaging architecture for AVEO that could organise a growing portfolio of vitamins, minerals, and combination formulas within a single, coherent visual system.

Inspired by the organisational logic of the periodic table, we developed a modular design language that allows products to be identified instantly while remaining visually connected. Large codes serve as the primary visual element, replacing the need for excessive labelling and reducing visual noise. Soft pastel gradients were introduced to create differentiation while preserving a calm, contemporary aesthetic.

The system was designed to support both single-ingredient products and combination formulas, enabling future product extensions without introducing new visual rules.

The result is a scalable packaging system that combines scientific structure with modern minimalism. By transforming nutrients into a clear visual language, the design creates instant recognition across the product range while maintaining a refined, premium brand experience.
